-----------------------------------------------------
Module registration macros:

module_init(ldd_moduleInit);
module_exit(ldd_moduleExit);

These tell the kernel which functions are 
the module's entry/exit functions.

------------------------------
Module metadata macros:

MODULE_LICENSE("GPL");
MODULE_AUTHOR("Lr");
MODULE_DESCRIPTION("...");

-----------------------------------------------------
                Your .c file
                    │
       ┌────────────┼─────────────┐
       ↓            ↓             ↓
   init.h       module.h      kernel.h
       │            │             │
       ↓            ↓             ↓
    __init      module_init()   pr_info()
    __exit      module_exit()
                 MODULE_*
				 

-----------------------------------------------------
Some names that look like functions are actually 
Linux kernel APIs, for example:

kmalloc(...)
kfree(...)
device_create(...)
device_destroy(...)

-----------------------------------------------------
__init and __exit are compiler/linker annotations 
implemented through macros.
