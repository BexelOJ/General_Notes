For your particular learning path, 
I would put the biggest emphasis on Ada packages → 
types → generics → tasking/protected objects → 
POSIX/Linux → C interoperability → SPARK → 
embedded → Yocto. 

That lets Ada become a systems-programming language 
in your existing Linux/embedded work rather than 
becoming a separate isolated language study.
