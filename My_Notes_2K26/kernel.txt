------------------------------------------------------------
atomic
    → protect simple atomic operations

spinlock
    → protect short critical sections
    → don't sleep while holding it

mutex
    → protect critical sections
    → can sleep

semaphore
    → counting/resource synchronization

rwlock
    → multiple readers OR one writer

wait queue
    → sleep until a condition becomes true

completion
    → wait for a specific operation/event to finish

workqueue
    → defer work to kernel worker threads

kthread
    → create your own kernel thread

delayed work
    → execute work later

tasklet
    → older deferred-execution mechanism
