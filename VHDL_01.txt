VHDL is strongly typed and more verbose, so it feels closer 
to a traditional programming language. 

It has:
------------------------------
Scalar types: 
bit, boolean, integer, real, etc.
------------------------------
Composite types:
Arrays – e.g., std_logic_vector(7 downto 0) for an 8-bit bus.

Records – like struct in C, grouping signals of different types.

------------------------------
Enumerated types: 
User-defined symbolic values, e.g. type state_type is 
(IDLE, RUN, DONE); for FSMs.

Access types (pointers): 
Rarely used in synthesis, mostly for testbenches.
