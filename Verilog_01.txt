Verilog is less strict, more hardware-centric:

------------------------------
Basic types: 
wire, reg, integer, real.

------------------------------
Arrays:

Packed arrays (bit vectors): 
reg [7:0] data;

Unpacked arrays: 
reg [7:0] memory [0:255]; (like RAM).

------------------------------
Structures & Unions (SystemVerilog extension):

struct and union allow grouping, similar to C.

------------------------------
Enums (SystemVerilog): 
Used for FSM states.
