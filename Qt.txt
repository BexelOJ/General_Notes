 History of Qt and QML
------------------------------
📜 Qt:

1991 – Originally developed by Haavard Nord and Eirik Chambe-Eng (Trolltech, Norway)

1995 – Qt 1.0 released (Linux, C++)

2000s – Widely adopted for KDE and cross-platform apps

2008 – Acquired by Nokia (Qt 4.x era)

2011 – Digia took over Qt from Nokia

2012 – Qt Project becomes open source under LGPL

Today – Owned and developed by The Qt Company

------------------------------
📜 QML:

Introduced in Qt 4.7 (2010) by Nokia

Became mature in Qt 5.x (with QtQuick 2)

Enables rapid UI prototyping with smooth animations

Used heavily in embedded/automotive and touchscreen apps
